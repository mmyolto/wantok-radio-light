Who we are

A Christian radio station for the whole nation.

Papua New Guinea is a country founded on Christian principles — written into the preamble of our Constitution. Wantok Radio Light exists to carry that foundation onto the airwaves, encouraging listeners, strengthening faith and serving churches and communities.

Our Mission

To preach Jesus Christ to as many Papua New Guineans as possible — in both urban and remote communities.

Our Vision

To use electronic media to spread the gospel across PNG and internationally, while addressing the social issues of our day.

    About Us

    Saturating the airwaves of PNG with the gospel.

    Wantok Radio Light is the voice of the PNG Christian Broadcasting Network — a Christian radio ministry serving Papua New Guinea since 2002.

    01

    Our Mission

    To preach Jesus Christ to as many Papua New Guineans as possible — people of all walks of life, in both urban and remote communities — and to saturate the airwaves of PNG with Christian programming.

    02

    Our Vision

    To use electronic communications media to spread the gospel of Jesus Christ to Papua New Guinea and internationally, while addressing social issues in partnership with churches and NGOs.

    Our History

    From a dream to the airwaves.

    The PNG Christian Broadcasting Network was established in 2001 and received approval from the PNG telecommunications authority (NICTA) to operate a Christian radio network.

    On January 14th, 2002 at 2:00pm, Wantok Radio Light launched its first ever Christian radio broadcast over the airwaves of Papua New Guinea.

    The broadcasting licence is held by the Papua New Guinea Bible Church, and the station is supported by many denominations and organisations — because we belong to the Body of Christ in PNG.

    See our journey
    Why we exist

    Integral human development — body, mind and spirit.

    Our national Constitution calls for the integral human development of every Papua New Guinean. We believe true development includes spiritual freedom — freedom from sin through Jesus Christ — alongside holistic personal growth.

    Wantok Radio Light exists to complement the work of the churches in the spiritual and social progress of our nation, reaching homes, villages and workplaces that a pulpit alone cannot reach.

    Core values

    What guides the ministry.

    Our work is shaped by faith in Christ, love for people, and a desire to serve PNG well.

    Faithfulness

    Encouragement

    Service

    Faithful on air since 2002.

    From a first broadcast in Port Moresby to shortwave across the nation — the story of what God has done through your giving.

    Tap a year to read its story

    2001

    The network is established

    PNG Christian Broadcasting Network is formed and approved by the national telecommunications authority to operate a Christian radio network.

    January 14th 2002

    Radio Light launches in Port Moresby

    At 2:00pm, Wantok Radio Light made its first-ever Christian radio broadcast over the airwaves of Papua New Guinea.

    2005

    A new home downtown

    The station relocated to the ground floor of Monian Tower in downtown Port Moresby.

    2007

    Land secured at Gerehu Stage 2

    The ministry secured its own land at Gerehu Stage 2 and renovated the facility that is still our home.

    November 2010

    Moving to satellite

    Wantok Radio Light migrated to a new satellite system and ended its EMTV service contract.

    2017

    80m tower at Burns Peak

    An 80-metre tower was built at Burns Peak to support Wantok Radio Light's operations.

    May 2019

    Shortwave moves to Mt. Hagen

    After vandalism at the 9 Mile site, our shortwave transmitter was re-installed at Teka, Western Highlands Province.

    2020

    Studio goes digital

    Our studio was upgraded from analog to digital.

    2023

    Solar-powered transmission

    Our pilot project for solar-powered transmission in Pangia was a success.

    2025

    85m tower in Lae

    An 85-metre tower was built in Lae to support Wantok Radio Light's operations.
